# ADR-NNNN: short statement of the decision, in the present tense

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date recorded:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Requirements:** the SRS IDs this decision implements or is constrained by
- **Related:** other ADRs

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the disagreement, or the thing that
would otherwise have been decided by accident. Written so that somebody who
disagrees with the outcome can still recognise the problem.

State the alternatives that were actually considered, not a strawman. An ADR
whose rejected options are obviously bad is an ADR nobody believes.

## Decision

One sentence, in the present tense, saying what is now true. Then the reasoning,
at whatever length it takes.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it forbids, and what would have to change to reverse it.
Include the consequences that are inconvenient. A record that lists only
benefits is marketing, and the next person to hit the cost will conclude the
decision was made without seeing it.
